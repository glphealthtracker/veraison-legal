Veraison — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 7, 2026 Last Updated: June 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Okiwaii LLC ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the Veraison mobile application (the "App"). It is incorporated by reference into our Terms of Use.

The short version: Veraison is built privacy-first. Everything you log — your routine, weigh-ins, moods, cycle markers, notes — is stored only on your device. We operate no servers, require no account, and cannot see, access, or sell your data. The only third party involved is the payment infrastructure when you choose to subscribe.

1. Information Stored on Your Device (Not Collected by Us)

The App lets you log wellness information such as medications, supplements, rituals, weigh-ins, moods, cycle phase, and related notes. This information is saved in a local database on your device and is never transmitted to us. We have no servers that receive it, no account system that links it to you, and no means of accessing it.

Because this data lives only on your device, it is protected by your device's own security (passcode, Face ID, encryption) and is included in your device backups according to your iOS settings.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • No account registration — no name, email, or password is requested by the App.
  • No analytics or behavioral tracking SDKs.
  • No advertising networks, ad identifiers, or cross-app tracking.
  • No location data.
  • No HealthKit data — the App does not read from or write to Apple Health.
  • No contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access.

We never use health or wellness information for advertising, marketing, or data mining, and we never sell or share personal data with data brokers.

3. Subscriptions and Payment Information

If you purchase a Premium subscription, the transaction is processed by Apple through your Apple ID. We never receive your payment card details.

To validate subscriptions, the App uses RevenueCat, a subscription-management service. RevenueCat receives a randomly generated anonymous identifier, purchase history for this App, and basic device information (such as device model and OS version). This identifier is not linked to your name or email by us. RevenueCat processes this information solely to manage subscription entitlements. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

4. Doctor Report Export

The App can generate a PDF summary of your logged stack for sharing with your healthcare provider. This PDF is created on your device, and sharing it (by mail, message, file save, or print) happens through the iOS share sheet under your control. We never receive a copy.

5. Notifications

If the App offers optional reminders, they are scheduled locally on your device. No push-notification server is used, and no notification data leaves your device.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your logged data remains on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual entries inside the App, or remove all App data at any time by deleting the App from your device. Because we hold no copy, deletion on your device is complete and permanent (apart from any device backups you control).

Subscription records held by Apple and RevenueCat are governed by their respective retention policies.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Laws such as the GDPR (EU/UK) and the CCPA/CPRA (California) give you rights over personal data a company holds about you — including access, correction, deletion, and portability. Because Veraison stores your data only on your device and we collect none of it, we generally hold no personal data about you to access, correct, or delete. The anonymous subscription identifier described in Section 3 can be deleted on request. To exercise any right or ask questions, contact us at veraisonapp@proton.me. We do not discriminate against anyone for exercising privacy rights.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has used the App in a way that concerns you, contact us at veraisonapp@proton.me.

9. Security

Your data is protected by your device's built-in security, including hardware encryption when a passcode is set. Because no data is transmitted to us, there is no server-side data to breach. We follow the principle of data minimization: the best-protected data is data that is never collected.

10. International Users

The App stores data locally wherever you are. Since we do not collect personal data, no cross-border transfer of your wellness information by us occurs.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be indicated by updating the "Last Updated" date and, where appropriate, by in-App notice. If a future version of the App ever introduced features that change our data practices (for example, optional cloud sync), we would update this Policy and ask for your consent where required before any such feature touches your data.

12. Contact

Questions about privacy: veraisonapp@proton.me — Okiwaii LLC, Texas, USA.
